## 2026-10-17 — Session 2 — P0-06 Postgres job queue: PASS
What changed
- migrations/versions/0003_job_queue.py: job table (PLAN.md columns + dedup_key unique,
  max_attempts, locked_by, last_error, created_at, finished_at; status check constraint;
  index on (status, run_after)).
- src/rlg/queue/jobs.py: enqueue (dedup_key -> idempotent), claim (UPDATE ... WHERE id =
  (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED LIMIT 1); also reclaims `running` jobs whose lock is
  older than lock_timeout; stale jobs already at max_attempts are failed instead),
  complete/fail guarded by locked_by (a reclaimed job ignores the dead worker's late
  writes), exponential backoff base*2^(n-1) capped at 6 h, stats.
- src/rlg/queue/worker.py: handler registry (@handler(kind)), run_worker loop, `noop` kind.
- CLI: `rlg queue work [--kind K] [--max-jobs N] [--once]`, `rlg queue stats`.
- tests/integration/test_queue.py: 4 threads x 200 jobs never double-claim; killed worker's
  job reclaimed only after lock timeout; retries with backoff then failed at max_attempts;
  worker loop retries a flaky handler; dedup.
Notes: worker death is simulated (claim without complete) with an injected clock rather
  than killing a real process; the claim transaction commits immediately, so the two are
  equivalent from the database's view.
